To My Love,

I made this because sometimes I don't know how to put everything I feel about our future into words.

I may not have everything figured out yet. I don't know exactly what business we'll build, where we'll eventually call home, or whether life will follow this plan perfectly.

But there is one thing I'm sure about.

I want to build it with you.

I want to grow into someone who can stand beside you, love you the way you deserve, support your dreams, and build a beautiful life with you.

I want Melbourne with you.

I want the quiet mornings with you, the late-night talks, the little laughs, and the comfort of simply being by your side.

I want our ordinary days, because even ordinary life feels special when it's with you.

I want our business, not just because I want success, but because I want to build something meaningful hand in hand with you.

I want our home, a place filled with peace, warmth, laughter, and the kind of love that always feels safe.

I want our wedding, the day I get to look at you and know I am marrying the love of my life.

I want our adventures, the big ones we dream about and the small ones we never plan for.

And more than anything, I want a life where no matter what changes around us, it is still you and me, loving each other, choosing each other, and growing together.

One day, I hope we look back at this little website and smile, because what started here as words, dreams, and feelings became our real life.

I don't need our future to be perfect.

I just want it to be ours — full of love, full of us, and full of all the beautiful moments we still have waiting ahead. LOVE YOU LOTS ma❤️
